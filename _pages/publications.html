My research focuses on reinforcement learning, statistical learning, and the evaluation of data-driven decision-making methods, with applications in healthcare.

Research Papers

Early Detection of Sepsis in Emergency Department and Prehospital Settings Using Artificial Intelligence: A Scoping Review

Selma Tabakovic, Ivana Pepic, Robert Feldt, Lars Ljungström, Daniel Dalevi, Lars-Magnus Andersson, Marina Axelson-Fisk, Katarina Bohm, and Stefan Candefjord
Under consideration at BMC Infectious Diseases

Reviews AI and machine learning approaches for early sepsis detection, examining methodological differences in model development, validation, and evaluation. Highlights limitations in external and prospective validation and challenges in translating predictive performance into clinical practice.

A Multi-Head Dueling Double Deep Q-Network Approach for Personalized Sepsis Treatment

Selma Tabakovic, Robert Feldt, and Marina Axelson-Fisk
Under consideration at BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making

Proposes a multi-head deep reinforcement learning architecture that combines shared representations with severity-specific treatment policies. Evaluates its ability to learn personalized treatment strategies from retrospective intensive-care data using off-policy evaluation.

A Controlled Simulation Framework for Ground-Truth Evaluation of Offline Reinforcement Learning in Clinical Decision Making

Selma Tabakovic, Robert Feldt, and Marina Axelson-Fisk
Manuscript in preparation

Develops a controlled simulation framework that enables reinforcement learning policies to be evaluated against known ground truth. Uses the framework to investigate treatment personalization, the influence of data-generating policies, and the reliability of offline policy evaluation.

Reliability of Off-Policy Evaluation in Clinical Reinforcement Learning: A Controlled Simulation Study

Selma Tabakovic, Robert Feldt, and Marina Axelson-Fisk
Manuscript in preparation

Investigates the reliability of off-policy evaluation under different data-generating policies, examining how errors in behavior-policy and value-function estimation, together with limited policy overlap, affect policy-value estimates and rankings.

Theses

Data-Driven Decision Support in Healthcare: Sequential Decision-Making and Policy Evaluation

PhD thesis · Chalmers University of Technology · In preparation

Investigates data-driven methods for clinical decision support, focusing on personalized treatment strategies using reinforcement learning and the challenges of evaluating their performance using retrospective data.

Co-clustering of Tensor Data Using Sparse Tensor Factorisation

Master's thesis · Chalmers University of Technology · 2020

Develops two co-clustering methods based on sparse tensor decomposition, using sign patterns and hierarchical clustering to identify structures in multidimensional data. Evaluates their performance and robustness through simulation studies and an application to genomic data.

Charge and Heat Control in Nanoelectronic Devices

Bachelor's thesis · Chalmers University of Technology · 2017

Investigates charge and heat transport in quantum-dot systems using master equations and scattering theory, focusing on thermoelectric properties and the control of heat and electrical currents.
