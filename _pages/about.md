I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics and Statistics at the [Department of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.chalmers.se/en/departments/mv/), a joint department of [Chalmers University of Technology](https://www.chalmers.se/en/) and the [University of Gothenburg](https://www.gu.se/en/). My doctoral research is funded by the [Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP)](https://wasp-sweden.org/). I am supervised by [Prof. Marina Axelson-Fisk](https://www.chalmers.se/en/persons/marinaa/) and co-supervised by [Prof. Robert Feldt](https://www.chalmers.se/en/persons/feldt/). I expect to defend my thesis in early 2027.

My research focuses on artificial intelligence and data-driven decision-making, particularly reinforcement learning and the challenges of evaluating decision-making methods using historical data. I am especially interested in understanding how reliable these methods are, what we can learn from observational data, and when we can trust their results. My research explores these questions in healthcare, with a focus on personalized decision-making and sepsis as a motivating application. My research explores these questions in healthcare, particularly in the context of personalized treatment strategies for sepsis.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss my research or get in touch!
